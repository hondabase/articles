# Honda Civic and Integra Valve Lash Inspection and Adjustment

Valve clearance that is too tight burns valves; too loose ticks and loses lift. Many Civic and Integra engines use screw-and-locknut rockers that are adjustable with feeler gauges.

## When to Adjust

- Audible tick after warm-up
- After head or cam work
- At the interval in the FSM
- After high mileage with unknown service history

## Conditions

- Engine cold unless your FSM explicitly states a hot spec for that code
- Correct intake vs exhaust clearances - never one value for all valves

## Procedure (screw / locknut)

1. Remove the valve cover; inspect for metal and cam wear.
2. Rotate the crank to the FSM starting position for cylinder 1.
3. Measure clearance with a clean feeler gauge at the specified interface.
4. Loosen the locknut, turn the adjuster to a light drag on the gauge, tighten the locknut, and re-check (clearance usually changes when locked).
5. Rotate to the next FSM position; repeat until all valves are done.
6. Reinstall the cover with a serviceable gasket; torque cover bolts evenly.

## Results

| After adjustment | Likely issue if still wrong |
| :--- | :--- |
| Still ticks | Flat cam, oiling problem, or clearance still large |
| Soft idle / low power | Clearance too tight |
| Locknuts loosen | Wrong torque or damaged threads |

If clearances open quickly again, stop adjusting and inspect the cam and rockers.
