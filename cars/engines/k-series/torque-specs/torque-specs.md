# Honda K-Series Torque Specifications & Rebuild Tolerances

This document provides factory torque values, bolt tightening sequences, and internal engineering clearances for Honda K-Series 2.0L and 2.4L engine platforms (K20A2, K20Z3, K24A2).

---

## Cylinder Head Torque Specifications & Sequence

The Honda K-Series uses yield bolts for cylinder head clamping. Ensure bolt threads and underside heads are lubricated with clean engine oil prior to assembly.

### Tightening Sequence Diagram
```
  [ 8 ]   [ 6 ]   [ 2 ]   [ 4 ]   [ 10 ]
  [ 9 ]   [ 3 ]   [ 1 ]   [ 5 ]   [ 7 ]
               (Front / Chain Side)
```

### Factory OEM Head Bolt Torque Steps
1.  **Step 1:** Torque all 10 bolts in sequence to **29 N·m (22 lb-ft / 3.0 kgf·m)**.
2.  **Step 2:** Tighten all bolts in sequence **90 degrees**.
3.  **Step 3:** Tighten all bolts in sequence an additional **90 degrees**.
4.  *If using new OEM bolts, execute an additional final 90-degree turn (total 270° angle).*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If installing **ARP 2000 Head Studs** (Part #208-4701), do not use the factory angle method. Torque ARP studs using ARP Ultra-Torque Lube in 3 equal steps up to a final torque of **80 lb-ft (108 N·m)**.

## Valve Lash Clearances (Cold Engine)

*   **Intake Valve Clearance:** `0.21 mm – 0.25 mm` (`0.008 in – 0.010 in`)
*   **Exhaust Valve Clearance:** `0.25 mm – 0.29 mm` (`0.010 in – 0.011 in`)
